Codex Project Backup

A personal backup utility operated by its owner. It creates encrypted copies of the owner’s software projects in a dedicated Google Drive account and retrieves those copies for recovery on a replacement computer.

The utility uses restic for encryption and versioned backups, and rclone for the owner-authorized Google Drive connection. It is for personal use and is not offered as a public service.

This website contains information only. There is no login, analytics, or upload form here.
